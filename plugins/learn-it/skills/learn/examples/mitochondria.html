How a mitochondrion makes ATP

Your cells run on ATP. Mitochondria make most of it by turning the energy in food into a pile of protons, then letting them flow back like water through a dam.

Try it in 10 minutes

In the Claude app. No biology background needed.

  1. Ask Claude: "Explain how mitochondria make ATP using a hydroelectric dam. Map water, dam wall, turbine and electricity to the real parts."
  2. Check its answer against the steps above. Protons should be the water, the inner membrane the dam wall, ATP synthase the turbine.
  3. Ask: "Where does the dam analogy break down?"
