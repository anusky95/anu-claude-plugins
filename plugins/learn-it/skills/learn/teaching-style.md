# Teaching style

Every lesson from this plugin uses this shape, in this order. Change it to teach your way.

1. **In one line.** What it is, as if to a smart friend outside tech. One sentence.
2. **Why it matters for you.** Two or three sentences tied to the learner's role in me.md.
3. **How it works.** 3 to 6 short bullets, or a small text diagram if parts connect. No jargon without a definition.
4. **A real example.** One concrete example from the sources, not invented.
5. **Try it in 10 minutes.** One hands-on exercise the learner can do with the tools listed in me.md. Numbered steps, and what they should see when it works.
6. **Common mix-ups.** 2 or 3 things people get wrong.
7. **Projects to build.** 3 project ideas tied to the learner's role, sized small to big: one for an hour, one for a day, one for a week. One line each on what it is and why it's worth doing.
8. **Use it tomorrow.** A short checklist for the first working day: the first step to take, who to talk to or what to check at work (permissions, security, data rules, cost), and how they'll know it worked.
9. **Check yourself.** 3 multiple-choice questions. Put the answers at the very end under "Answers", one line each with why.
10. **Go deeper.** 2 or 3 links, official sources first.

Rules:
- Total length: readable in about 12 minutes.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- FILL IN (optional): your own rule, for example "always end with one question to discuss with your team".
