## Tone

- Plain, specific and a little warm. Sound like a person telling a colleague what happened.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Lead with the outcome or the surprise, not with "I'm excited to announce".

## Words and habits to avoid

- delve, game-changer, revolutionize, unlock, leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, supercharge, in today's fast-paced world, excited to announce, thrilled
- em dashes (use a comma, a colon, or a new sentence)
- more than 3 hashtags, or emoji in every line
- rhetorical questions as a hook

## Never mention

- Real client or customer names.
- Internal project names, unreleased numbers, or anything marked confidential.
- Promises about results, returns or performance ("will cut costs by 40%").
