# Formats

Each format has a limit and a shape. To add a format, copy a block, give it a new name, and say whether it is a default.

## LinkedIn post (default)

- 80 to 200 words.
- Line 1 is the hook: the outcome, the surprise or the lesson, in under 15 words.
- Then 2 to 4 short paragraphs: what it is, what happened, what you learned.
- End with one plain line that invites a reply or points to a link. No "Thoughts?".
- 0 to 3 hashtags on the last line.

## Slack or Teams announcement (default)

- 3 lines at most.
- Line 1: what shipped or happened, starting with the outcome.
- Line 2: who it helps and how to try it.
- Line 3: where to ask questions or the link.

## Email blurb

- Subject line under 8 words.
- 4 sentences at most: what, why it matters to the reader, how to try it, who to contact.

## Release notes (default when the source is code or commits)

- A heading with the version or date. Use `Needs input` if neither is known.
- Sections in this order, leaving out any that are empty: New, Improved, Fixed, Heads up.
- One line per item, written for a user, not for the developer. Say what changed for them.
